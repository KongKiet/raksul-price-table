## Goal

Add pointer-hover feedback for available price cells: strongly highlight the hovered cell and weakly highlight its matching quantity row and business-day column. Keep hover identity independent from persistent selection, support expanded rows and sparse response ordering, and clear hover synchronously when a different paper size is applied.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Pointer entry on an available price strongly highlights exactly that price cell and weakly highlights its corresponding quantity row and business-day column, including the applicable row and column headers.
2. Pointer exit removes all transient row, column, and cell hover highlighting without changing persistent selection or Order price.
3. Moving directly between available cells transfers all hover highlighting to the new quantity and business-day identity without stale highlights.
4. Hover identity uses numeric quantity and business day and follows existing response mapping, never price value or array position; reordered entries and equal prices remain correct.
5. Unavailable combinations cannot initiate a hover identity and retain their unavailable presentation when another cell highlights their row or column.
6. Hover works for rows revealed by See more and does not alter expansion state, issue a request, or change visible row count.
7. Hover remains independent from selection: hovering another cell does not replace the selected cell, and selection remains after movement and exit.
8. When hover and selection overlap, both states remain understandable; transient highlighting does not erase the persistent selected boundary or visible keyboard focus.
9. Draft-only changes and same-size Apply preserve hover and cause no request. Applying a different paper size clears hover synchronously, and stale request settlement cannot restore it.
10. Styling is distinct at desktop and narrow widths, works with striped rows and sticky headers, and preserves semantic structure, control names, keyboard operation, and contained scrolling.
11. Controlled tests cover pointer entry, exit, movement, row/column/cell targeting, reordered and sparse data, selection independence, expanded rows, and paper-size preservation/reset without a live API.
12. The final diff is scoped; acceptance criteria and the task diff are inspected; focused/full tests, formatter tests, formatting, lint, and build pass; latest Chrome or Firefox verifies the interaction and responsive presentation.

## Implementation assumptions

These task-level choices are separate from assignment requirements:

- Store hoveredCell in App as a nullable PriceCellIdentity, independent from selectedCell, so different-size Apply can clear both synchronously.
- Pass hover identity plus entry and exit callbacks into PriceTable. Available buttons set identity on pointer entry and clear it on pointer exit; unavailable cells remain noninteractive.
- A row is weakly highlighted when quantity matches, a column is weakly highlighted when business day matches, and the exact available cell receives the strong highlight.
- Apply column state to its header and body cells and row state to its header and body cells. The exact cell has visual precedence where weak states intersect.
- Preserve selected styling as a persistent inset boundary and focus styling as a non-color outline and underline.
- Guard pointer exit by the departing identity so a late leave cannot clear a newer hovered cell.
- Draft-only changes and same-size Apply preserve hover. A different-size Apply clears hover immediately before the old table leaves.

## Risks and blockers

- Pure CSS cannot robustly derive the corresponding column across sparse, reordered data; use explicit identity state and existing mapping.
- Weak backgrounds may be hidden by stripes or sticky-header backgrounds. Use explicit scoped classes and verify cascade order.
- Broad row handlers could strongly highlight unavailable cells or retain state while moving within a row. Initiate identity only from available buttons.
- Unconditional pointer exit can clear a newer hover under overlapping events. Clear only when the leaving identity still owns state.
- Strong hover can visually erase selection or focus. Preserve the selected boundary and focus outline/underline above background rules.
- Effect-based reset can expose stale hover during Apply. Clear synchronously in the existing transition.
- The missing reference prevents exact visual comparison; requirements-based browser QA remains required.
- Prior-task shipping records overlap docs/plan.md. Preserve their hunks and stage only T06-owned content during shipping.

No unresolved blocker prevents implementation.
